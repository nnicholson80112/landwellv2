Questions

Before you switch

Does anyone come to my property?

No. Landwell is software. Showings are self-guided through a lock you control, or you show the home yourself. Repairs go to your own vendors.

Who decides which applicant gets the home?

You do. Landwell collects applications and shows the screening report next to your written criteria, the same way for everyone. It never scores, ranks or recommends people.

Can I use my own lease?

Yes. Upload it once. Landwell fills in each new tenant's names, dates, rent and deposit and sends it for e-signature. Your wording stays yours.

Does Landwell touch my rent money?

Never. Tenants pay you directly, the same way they do today. Landwell sends the reminders and late notices, and you mark rent received with one tap. Colorado requires you to offer tenants a free way to pay, such as Zelle or a check.

What if a tenant has an emergency at 2 AM?

Landwell answers right away, dispatches your emergency vendor and texts you. If anyone is in danger, it tells them to call 911 first.

I already have a property manager. How do I switch?

We give you a letter to end your current agreement and a checklist for getting tenant records and deposits back. Most owners are running in about a week.

Keep the rent. Lose the busywork.
